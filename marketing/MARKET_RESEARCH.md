## 1. Market Overview & Macro Trends

The "Doku" and daily grid-puzzle genre—ignited originally by *Immaculate Grid* (sports) and popularized across gaming fandoms by *PokeDoku* (Pokemon) and *Costodoku*—represents one of the most cost-effective organic acquisition formats in modern web gaming.

### Key Dynamics of the Genre
1. **Ritualistic Daily Retention:** Players integrate daily puzzle games into their morning or commute routines. A single completed puzzle often converts into a multi-month daily habit if streak mechanics and calendar resets are seamless.
2. **Social Score Sharing:** The emoji-grid format pioneered by *Wordle* enables players to boast accomplishments on Discord, Reddit, and group chats without spoiling specific answers.
3. **The "Rarity" Mechanic as the True Endgame:** For seasoned fans, simply solving the 9 cells is too easy. The true competitive thrill comes from achieving the lowest possible obscurity percentage (picking forgotten or obscure characters rather than obvious poster brawlers like Colt, Shelly, or Spike).

## 3. Audience Personas & Search Intent

### Persona A: "The Daily Fandom Commuter"
- **Demographics:** Students & young adults (ages 13–28), mobile-first.
- **Behavior:** Plays 1–3 daily browser games during transit or downtime (e.g., Wordle, Connections, PokeDoku).
- **Search Intent:** `"brawl stars daily puzzle"`, `"daily brawl stars game"`, `"brawldle alternative"`.
- **Primary Driver:** Quick 2-minute daily mental stimulation + streak maintenance.

### Persona B: "The Brawl Stars Encyclopedia / Hardcore Fan"
- **Demographics:** Active competitive and casual players with 20k+ trophies.
- **Behavior:** Knows release years, ammo counts, gadget specifics, and lore trivia.
- **Search Intent:** `"brawl stars trivia game"`, `"brawler quiz"`, `"hardest brawl stars test"`.
- **Primary Driver:** Proving superior knowledge and hunting ultra-rare answers (obscurity score flexing).

### Persona C: "The Friend Group Competitor"
- **Demographics:** School, club, or clan members playing Brawl Stars together.
- **Behavior:** Shares screenshots and emoji grids in Discord servers and group chats.
- **Search Intent:** Driven by peer referrals ("Check out my score: 8/9!").
- **Primary Driver:** Beating friends' scores and boasting rights.

## 4. Discovery Channels: Evaluation & Evidence

### Channel 1: Dedicated Daily Game Directories (Priority: Highest)
- **Platforms Researched:**
  1. **DailyDoku** (`https://daily-doku.com`): Curates daily browser games with custom routines and local storage. Features an open "Suggest a game" submission queue.
  2. **Playlin** (`https://playlin.io`): Directory for daily games with an active "Submit your game" pipeline.
  3. **Dailydles** (`https://dailydles.com`): Hand-checked directory of free-to-play daily browser games with no login barriers.
  4. **Listdle** (`https://listdle.com`): Free submission portal for high-quality daily browser games.
  5. **Alldle** (`https://alldle.net`): Directory covering daily and unlimited browser puzzles.
- **Evidence:** Daily puzzle enthusiasts actively use these aggregators to build their personal daily routines. Submitting is free, takes minimal effort, and creates permanent, relevant referral streams.

### Channel 2: Reddit Gaming Communities (Priority: High)
- **Target Subreddits:**
  - `r/Brawlstars` (1.8M+ members): Passionate community hungry for original fan creations.
    - *Policy Check:* Rule 6 strictly regulates self-promotion. Link-dropping is banned. Original, high-effort fan art and interactive projects are warmly welcomed if framed as community discussions with full disclosure under Supercell's Fan Content Policy.
  - `r/BrawlStarsCompetitive` (120k+ members): Focuses on mechanics, stats, and meta. Responds well to tricky category debates (e.g., brawler projectile types, reload tiers).
  - `r/WebGames` (600k+ members): Dedicated to browser games that require no downloads, no paywalls, and no account walls. Brawldoku meets 100% of these criteria.
- **Evidence:** Similar indie fan games regularly generate tens of thousands of initial plays from a single high-effort Reddit launch post that reaches the front page.

### Channel 3: Video Content Creators (Priority: Medium–High)
- **Creators:** CarnageGame, Lex, Rey, KairosTime, Tribe Gaming, OJ, SpenLC.
- **Format:** "Can I beat the hardest Brawl Stars trivia grid?", "Impossible Brawl Stars category test", or live-streaming morning warmup puzzles.
- **Evidence:** Short-form quiz videos ("Guess the Brawler by X") routinely garner 100k–1M+ views on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Brawldoku provides an instant, ready-made video concept for creators looking for interactive challenge content.
