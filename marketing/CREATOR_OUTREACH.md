## 1. Executive Summary & Campaign Strategy

Daily grid puzzles like PokeDoku, Immaculate Grid, and Wordle achieved exponential viral adoption when video creators began playing them as daily warm-ups, speedruns, and community challenge videos. 

In the *Brawl Stars* creator ecosystem, short-form trivia ("Guess the Brawler in 60 Seconds", "Guess by Voice Line", "Guess by Emoji", and "Brawldle Speedruns") routinely achieves high engagement (10K–250K+ views). Brawldoku provides these creators with a **turnkey, highly replayable content asset**:
1. **Zero Prep Time:** Creators do not need to invent questions or design slides—the daily puzzle is ready to play.
2. **Viewer Engagement:** Chat and comment sections flood with viewers arguing over valid picks and obscure brawler choices.
3. **Competitive Hook:** The new **Rarity-Based Scoring System** (Legendary picks = 200 pts, Common = 110 pts) allows creators to compete for high scores, speedrun clears, or hunt for 0% obscurity brawlers.
