  > - It calculates community obscurity percentages in real-time, so you can see if you picked the obvious brawler or an obscure sleeper pick.
  > - A brand new board generates every day at midnight UTC.
  > 
  > It is 100% free, has no ads, requires no download or account, and saves your progress right in your browser.
